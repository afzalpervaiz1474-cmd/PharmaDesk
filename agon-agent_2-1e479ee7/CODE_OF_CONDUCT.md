# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

PharmaDesk is built for people who run pharmacies and by people who want to help them. Everyone who takes part
in this project — in issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews — is expected to help keep it a respectful,
professional and welcoming place, regardless of experience level, background, nationality, language, gender,
age, disability, religion or any other personal characteristic.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate. Assume good intent and ask before assuming.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Explain your reasoning.
- Accept review comments gracefully, and offer them constructively.
- Be patient with newcomers and with people writing in a second language.
- Be honest about what your contribution does, what you tested and what you did not.
- Respect privacy. Never post another person's private information, and never share real customer, patient or
  store data in issues, logs or screenshots.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, threats or sustained disruption.
- Insults, personal attacks, or demeaning comments about any group or individual.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome advances.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Knowingly submitting malicious code, or misrepresenting what a contribution does.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces and whenever someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the project maintainers using the
contact details on the repository owner's GitHub profile, or use GitHub's "Report content" option on the
comment or profile concerned. Do not respond in kind in the public thread.

Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers who have a conflict of interest in a report will step back from
handling it.

> Maintainers: if you set up a dedicated contact address for conduct reports, add it here.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will review every report and respond in the way they judge fair and proportionate:

1. **Correction** — a private note explaining what was inappropriate and what is expected.
2. **Warning** — a formal warning with consequences for continued behaviour.
3. **Temporary restriction** — a time-limited ban from interacting with the project.
4. **Permanent ban** — for serious or repeated violations.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not
follow this Code of Conduct.
